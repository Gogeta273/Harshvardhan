GAME 02 · PC · UNITY
PRISON ESCAPE

"Collect the key. Dodge the cones. Break free."

A 3D isometric stealth puzzle game where you play as a thief trapped inside a maze patrolled by police officers. Navigate through 5 escalating levels — collect keys, dodge vision cones, use your invisibility cloak, and escape before they catch you.

PC WINDOWS UNITY 3D ISOMETRIC STEALTH PUZZLE VISION CONE AI
GENRE
3D Isometric Stealth
ENGINE
Unity
PLATFORM
PC (Windows)
CORE LOOP
Observe → Dodge → Escape
// CORE MECHANICS
MOVEMENT — WASD / Arrow keys in a fixed isometric 3D perspective
VISION CONE — Police have 180° dynamic cones. Yellow = patrol. Red = chase triggered
SHADOW CLOAK — 5s invisibility. Break line-of-sight to end a chase
OBJECTIVE — Collect key(s) / scrolls, reach the exit without being caught
// TARGET AUDIENCE
Casual to mid-core players aged 13+. Fans of Monaco and Hello Neighbor — a blend of stealth, maze exploration, and strategy.
// LEVEL PROGRESSION — 5 LEVELS
LEVEL 01 — THE CELL
Small maze with one key in the centre. Guards patrolling. Learn to observe patrol patterns before every move.
LEVEL 02 — THE YARD
Bigger, more complex maze. More guards. Shadow Cloak available — 5s invisibility. Use it wisely.
LEVEL 03 — AGAINST THE CLOCK ⏱
Countdown timer added. Collect all 3 keys before time expires — while dodging guards.
LEVEL 04 — THE PADLOCK 🔒
Cell locked with a combination. Collect scrolls to decode it, unlock the cell, grab the key — guards everywhere.
LEVEL 05 — SPOTLIGHT ⚠ FINAL
Massive prison searchlights sweep the level. Step into the light and every guard converges on you.
// USP
Dynamic vision cones that shift yellow → red on detection. Forces players to read patrol patterns, plan movements, and use abilities strategically.
// ART & TECH
Low-poly 3D  ·  Fixed Cinemachine isometric cam  ·  Real-time spotlight shadows
Dynamic vision cone AI  ·  Modular maze design  ·  Alert SFX + ambient audio
